TXT-001 minor text confirmed · static Terminology and consistency

The swarm is called 'bees' in some texts and 'bugs' in others, even in two consecutive How to Play cards

Expected

One term for the creatures the player sends out. The game has 6 bug types (Bee, Ladybug, Beetle, Butterfly, Ant, Firefly) and a 'Bugs' collection tab, so 'bugs' is the general term; 'bees' is only right when the Bee is equipped.

Actual

How to Play card 2 says 'bees fly out of the tree', card 3 says 'bugs enter the picture'. The tutorial and three toasts say 'bees'; the lock toast and a notification say 'bugs'.

Evidence

Suggested fix: Use 'bugs' in all general texts (e.g. 'bugs fly out of the tree', 'Bugs eat from the bottom up', 'wait for the bugs'). Optionally insert the equipped bug's name where the sentence is about the current swarm.

TXT-002 minor text confirmed · 3/3 Text in context (layout, overlap, truncation)

Tutorial step dots are drawn on top of the ad banner text at the bottom of the game screen

Environment: Mobile viewport 390x844 @2x, fresh tutorial (#tutorial)

Preconditions: Tutorial not seen yet (new player)

Steps
  1. Start the first level as a new player (or open the build with #tutorial)
  2. Look at the bottom of the screen while the first tutorial bubble is shown
Expected

The six tutorial step dots sit in free space and the banner text stays readable

Actual

The dots are painted over the middle of the banner text on every run, whichever banner slide is showing (Halloween Event on run 1, Hard Mode on run 2, Gallery on run 3)

Evidence

Suspected cause (hypothesis): Likely the dots are positioned a fixed 14px from the bottom of the screen, which is inside the 50px ad banner (.ad-banner, css/style.css:761).

Suggested fix: Place the dots inside the tutorial bubble, or offset them above the banner (bottom: banner height + 14px); hide the banner during the tutorial if that is acceptable.

TXT-003 minor text confirmed · 3/3 Text in context (layout, overlap, truncation)

Store: the No Ads subtitle is cramped and leaves 'ads' alone on a second line on phone widths

Environment: Mobile viewport 390x844 and 360x740

Steps
  1. Open the Store from the bottom navigation
  2. Look at the No Ads card under the Special Offer
Expected

'Remove banner and video ads' readable, letters not touching, no single-word orphan line

Actual

At 390px the outline makes letters touch and 'ads' wraps alone; at 360px it wraps after 'and' and stays cramped

Evidence

Suspected cause (hypothesis): A 3px text stroke on 13px text eats the space between letters; the card title area is narrow next to the price button.

Suggested fix: Reduce the stroke for the small subtitle (1.5 to 2px) or drop it, and shorten the copy to 'No banner or video ads'.

TXT-004 trivial text confirmed · static Terminology and consistency

Booster descriptions say 'in line' where How to Play says 'in the line', and describe Shuffle differently

Expected

Shuffle: 'Mixes the colors of the blocks in the line.' Free Pick: 'Pick any block in the line and send it to a free slot.'

Actual

'Changes the colors of the blocks in line.' and 'Pick any block in line...' (missing 'the'; 'changes' vs 'mixes' in How to Play)

Evidence

Suggested fix: Mixes the colors of the blocks in the line. / Pick any block in the line and send it to a free slot.

TXT-005 trivial text confirmed · static Terminology and consistency

'Out of Slots' is capitalised mid-sentence in How to Play but lowercase in the tutorial

Expected

The same casing for the same term in running text (the popup title 'Out of Slots!' can stay Title Case)

Actual

How to Play: 'you're Out of Slots.'; tutorial: 'you're out of slots and you lose.'

Evidence

Suggested fix: Pick one: keep the highlighted 'Out of Slots' as a named state in both places, or lowercase it in How to Play.

TXT-006 trivial text confirmed · static Terminology and consistency

Two ellipsis styles: 'Loading...' (three dots) vs 'Sending…' and 'Taking a screenshot…'

Expected

One ellipsis style across the game

Actual

The loading screen uses '...'; the Report popup (Settings > Report) uses the '…' character

Evidence

Suggested fix: Loading…

QRY-001 query confirmed Terminology and consistency

New Feature card titles mix plural and singular names

Actual

Plural: Mystery Blocks, Frozen Blocks, Hidden Pixels, Linked Blocks, Mystery Links. Singular: Big Pixel, Pipe, Key & Lock, Wood Block, Cookie Blocker (5 vs 5, no majority style).

Evidence

Suggested fix: If a rule is wanted: plural for all (Big Pixels, Pipes, Keys & Locks, Wood Blocks, Cookie Blockers). Design decision, not a defect.
