BUG-001 major bug confirmed · 12/12 Win flow and rewards

Level Complete popup closes on a tap outside it or Escape: the coin reward is never paid and the player is left on an empty board

Environment: Web, 390x844 touch; 4 runs (2 tap outside, 2 Escape) in each of 3 sessions

Preconditions: Returning player with 100 coins

Steps
  1. Win Flowers Easy level 1 (Level Complete shows '+30')
  2. Tap anywhere outside the popup card (or press Escape on a keyboard)
Expected

The popup stays open until Continue or Claim x2 is chosen (like the Out of Slots popup, which is locked), or closing it pays the reward and moves on

Actual

The popup closes, coins stay at 100 (the +30 is lost), the board is empty and there is no Continue button. The win itself is saved (3 stars), so the player can only leave through Pause.

Evidence

Suspected cause (hypothesis): popup-complete lacks the data-locked attribute, so the generic outside-tap and Escape handlers (app.js:237, 3028) close it without calling finishWin().

Suggested fix: Add data-locked to #popup-complete (index.html:423), as #popup-fail already has.

BUG-002 major bug confirmed · 3/3 Phone portrait (360, 390, 412 px)

Top bar overflows on 360-390 px phones: the Settings gear (menu) and the Close X (store) are cut or pushed off screen, completely from 9,999 coins

Environment: Web, touch, widths 360 / 390 / 412 px, 15 balance combinations per run (2 runs) + a recorded store session (run 3). Both balances count: the bar grows with gems and coins

Steps
  1. Open the game on a 360 px wide phone (a very common Android width) with 9,999 coins
  2. Look at the top-right corner of the main menu, then open the Store
Expected

Level, gems, coins and the Settings gear (menu) / Close X (store) all fit on screen at any balance

Actual

360 px: gear 29 of 42 px visible at the starting balance, 0 px (fully off screen) at 9,999 and 98.8K/123.5K coins; the store has no visible Close X. 390 px: gear cut from 9,999 coins. 412 px: only the store X slightly cut at 98.8K/123.5K.

Evidence

Suspected cause (hypothesis): The top-bar pills have fixed minimum widths and the row does not shrink or wrap below ~400 px; numbers from 1,000 to 9,999 are shown in full (formatNumber only abbreviates from 10,000), so they are the widest case.

Suggested fix: Let the currency pills shrink (min-width: 0, smaller font under 400 px) and abbreviate from 1,000 (1.2K); keep the gear/X outside the flexible area so it can never be pushed out.

BUG-003 major bug confirmed · 4/4 Ownership (No Ads, Special Offer)

Store keeps selling the $4.99 Special Offer, which includes No Ads, to players who already own No Ads

Environment: Web, 390x844 touch; 2 runs in each of 2 sessions

Preconditions: No Ads owned (bought alone for $2.99 or in an earlier offer)

Steps
  1. Open the Store
  2. Look at the Special Offer card
  3. Tap $4.99
Expected

The offer is hidden once No Ads is owned, or shown without No Ads at a new price (the No Ads card already shows 'Owned')

Actual

The card still lists '1,200 gems · 10,000 coins · No Ads' for $4.99 and can be bought again; the toast says 'No Ads' was granted again. With real payments this charges for a non-consumable the player owns.

Evidence

Suspected cause (hypothesis): renderStore() builds the offer card unconditionally, while maybeShowOffer() already checks state.noAds.

Suggested fix: Hide the offer card when state.noAds is true (same rule as the popup), or show a variant without No Ads.

BUG-004 minor bug confirmed · 5/5 Slot limits

Add Slot from the Out of Slots popup has no maximum: repeated fails grow the board to 10 squeezed slots (the booster stops at 7)

Environment: Web, 390x844 touch; 5 runs over 3 sessions. Fails triggered with the debug 'Lose level' (assisted); Add Slot and the ad are the real flow

Steps
  1. Start Flowers Easy level 1 (4 slots)
  2. Run out of slots, tap 'Add Slot' on the Out of Slots popup and watch the 3 s ad
  3. Run out of slots again and repeat 6 times
Expected

Same limit as the Add Slot booster: 'Maximum slots reached' at 7 slots

Actual

Slots go 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10. At 10 they shrink to thin pills across the full width and the first slot's block is wider than its neighbours.

Evidence

Suspected cause (hypothesis): The fail-popup handler pushes a slot without the 7-slot check that useBooster('add_slot') has.

Suggested fix: Apply the same limit in 'fail-addslot' and hide or disable the Add Slot button on the fail popup at 7 slots.
